# TESTER report v3.3 (app.js?v=3.3, live, 844x390)

**Fun 7.5/10** (was 6.5). The campaign finally has an arc: a disaster, a recovery, a clean win, then a night that goes down to the wire.

**Difficulty verdict: NOT too easy for a real player. It is HARD at the start, then correctly tight.** DESIGNER's no-injury Dawn needs perfect play. I went in cold with all six tired and no recon and was wiped in Mission 1.

## Chapter stats
- Length: Day 12 to Day 22 (11 days), roughly 40 min wall time. Combat pauses on every event, so it ran slower than the sim clock.
- Missions: Shop 2/6 saved (squad overrun, Mike KIA, 4 others dragged out for 2-3 days). Cold Storage 5/5 saved (3 then 2). Long Night 4/6 safe.
- Deaths: 2 (Mike, Ruiz). David wounded. Joel scar 1/3.
- Ammo: 400 at start, 74 after the Shop wipe. 36 rds left at the end (fired 217 on the night).
- Dawn arrived with shelter 33%, 10 seconds to go, Ruiz dead.

## Was the Long Night tense?
Yes, very. Shelter 100% to breached in about 40s. Then a push-or-stay call on 6 exposed civilians, a bloater, Ruiz down, and 36 rounds with 10s to dawn. Patch up failed ("it didn't take"), which was cruel but fair. This is the best part of the game.

## Upgrades
Real choice. 1 build a day with 2-4 parts covers Walls 4, Bench 3, Mast 2, Drill 2, Infirmary 3, so I could not have everything. I picked Mast, Bench, Drill, Walls and skipped Infirmary, and missed it when Ruiz bled out. Walls felt required, so make that part less of a given.

## Text
Mostly clear. The brief, the intel and the "who comes out" cards are good. Problems are listed below.

## What's fun
- Road events with a gamble (Slip past 40%, Check it).
- Timed prompts: Seal door, Take 3 vs all, Focus fire on a bloater.
- Ammo burn and the Night needs ~250 line, which makes scavenging a real risk.

## What's flat
- Scavenge came back "hurt" 3 out of 3 times, so it is a tax, not a gamble.
- Rest/Scavenge is the same call every day.
- Defensive stance never felt different.

## Bugs, phone first
1. The sim pauses after nearly every event/log line and the button then shows a play icon, so I re-tapped play dozens of times. The UI says "paused" with no hint it is automatic. On a phone this is the main friction. Is it throttled while the tab is hidden, or intended?
2. Mission 1 wipe: tired squad, 0 rds in about 70s of real play, 4 of 6 incapacitated at once. The game gave no warning that "all tired" was risky.
3. Dawn card says "4/6 safe" but the ending screen counts 4 saw dawn and 7 saved in total. "5 missions" is wrong (I deployed 3 times).
4. Header shows "Day 22" while the log still says "Day 21".
5. The map node panel for Cold Storage shows "RESOLVED" on the other node while Cold Storage is still open. Easy to misread. Long Night stayed "Locked" with no hint it needs Cold Storage fully cleared.
6. "Take 3 / Take all" shows "3 shooting" ambiguously, and the noise cap jumps 30 to 60 to 100 without comment.
7. The ending, rotated 390x844, renders turned 90 degrees but fits. No console errors.

## Notes for DESIGNER (ranked)
1. Mission 1 is a cliff. Warn on "all tired" at deploy, or cut the ammo burn (210 rds gone in about 60s).
2. Scavenge needs real variance (some clean returns) or a different cost.
3. Add a heal/recover option for the medic so a down man is not lost to a 38% patch.
4. Show the unlock condition for the Long Night.
5. The Long Night is great, so protect it.

## Notes for UI
1. Auto-pause: show the reason and add auto-resume or a 1-tap resume that stays at 2x.
2. Fix the day counter, mission count and ending totals.
3. Put a "when you can act" cue on the command dots.
